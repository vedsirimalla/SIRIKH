          Set monthly budget

          Sets the joint budget for the month of the start date. Every joint expense in that month counts, even ones logged before. Budgets reset on the 1st; nothing carries over.

          Set personal budget

          Sets the budget for the month of the start date. Every Personal expense for that person in that month comes out of it, whoever's card paid, even ones logged before. Leave a box empty to keep that person's current budget.

          Record a settlement

          Money paid back to clear what is owed (Splitwise, cash...). It is not an expense and does not touch any budget.

          Savings goal

          Investments (401(K), India, USA...) count toward the goal, split equally: each of you aims for half. Surplus counts once you invest it.

          Already invested before tracking (from the Fam. tab). Added once and counted toward the goal.

          Savings target

          The share of each month's income to keep (investments plus savings). Safe to spend = expected income minus this share minus what is already spent, loan payments included.

          Weekly email

          Sent every Sunday evening from your Google account. Addresses are kept in the sheet's script settings, not in the app.
